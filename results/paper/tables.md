# Paper tables

Generated by `src/paper_tables.py` from stored results; the CSV files hold the full tables. MASE and CRPS are scaled by the seasonal-naive scale at each origin. Weighted means use the 2022 mean per-day emissions of each series and are computed within one level at a time (system economies; World series one by one). Main window: target months to 2025-12 without 2025-09, origins 2022-12 to 2025-06.

## T1c. Main data issues and how they are handled

File: `results/paper/T1c_issues.csv`.

| Data issue | How it is handled |
|---|---|
| Missing component values where the published totals identify them as zero | Structural zeros (every published total containing the value adds up without it); logged; nothing else filled |
| Published residence total differs from the component sum | System total = component sum A+B+D+E+F; the published total is scored with the forecast divided by (1 - d) |
| E(World) + F(World) differs from G(World) by about 0.1% | Discrepancy node DELTA_INT; in reconciliation the ratio r = DELTA_INT / G(World) enters the balance row |
| OECD aggregate is not the sum of its members (territory) | Group totals built from economies |
| September 2025 spike (suspected processing artefact) | flag_sep2025: every evaluation with and without it |
| 2022 domestic/international episode, 2021-12 to 2022-10: World international share 74.1% (same months a year earlier) to 65.5% (minimum 64.4% in 2022-06); B = C +188 kt/day, F -167, G -173; World totals +2.5% | Documented (DATA_RULES.md, Fig. 1); 11 of the 13 long-horizon origins (2021-12 to 2022-12) lie inside it, which affects the reading of the skill estimates for components and territory measures; diagnostic skill of the totals in results/episode_2022/skill_totals.csv |
| 2026 domestic/international reclassification (about 300 kt/day by June 2026) | Main evaluation window ends 2025-12; 2026-01 to 2026-06 is a separate stress test |
| Series that are zero for two years | Fixed at zero at that origin, still scored |
| Switzerland: A, B, F unpublished for 2025 | Outside the system; published residence total forecast as an outside-system series |
| Territory assignment of ports (e.g. Malaysia high, Hong Kong low) | Not handled; reported as a limitation (DATA_RULES.md) |
| Components published to 2026-06, residence totals to 2026-09 | The test at origin 2026-06 scores the published residence totals of 2026-07 to 2026-09 |
